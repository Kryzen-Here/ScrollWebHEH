Emotional Instability

The amygdala—the brain's emotional center—becomes 60% more reactive. You lose the ability to regulate stress and irritation.

After 1 Week

Systemic Breakdown

Your immune system's T-cell production drops. Stress hormones like cortisol spike, keeping your body in a constant state of "fight or flight".

Inflammation levels rise across the body, increasing the risk of cardiovascular strain.
Long-Term Impact

Cognitive Decline

Chronic sleep debt is linked to neurodegenerative risks. Your brain's "glymphatic" cleaning system cannot clear toxic proteins effectively.

Burnout isn't just a feeling; it's a physiological state where your systems begin to prioritize survival over function.

Your body keeps score.

Sleep is not a luxury. It is a biological necessity. You cannot "catch up" on debt—you can only choose to invest in your future self.
